title: Get started with Pidgeon (the launcher)
description: One sign-in, one place to see which Pidgeon apps are installed, what version they're on, and what your account includes.
---

Pidgeon is the launcher app: one sign-in and a single grid of your Pidgeon products, whether each is installed, what your account entitles you to, and a button to open each one. The grid covers Post, Flock, Loft, and Migrate today; Conform joins it as its desktop app comes out of beta. You don't need the launcher to use any individual app, but it's the fastest way to see everything at once.

## Before you start

- [Install Pidgeon](/getting-started/install-windows) (or on [macOS](/getting-started/install-macos)).

## One loop: sign in, see your products, launch one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Launch Pidgeon and sign in with your Pidgeon account. The launcher requires sign-in; there is no anonymous mode here (Post is the app that works without an account).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read the product grid">
    The **Products** view shows a card per product. Each card carries:

    - Installation status: **Installed**, **Not installed**, or **Checking…** while it resolves
    - An entitlement badge showing what your account includes for that product
    - **Open** when the app is installed and entitled
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch an app">
    Click **Open** on an installed product, or press <kbd>mod</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> for the command palette, arrow to a product, and hit <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check versions and updates">
    The status bar shows the launcher version. Product cards show installed versions.

    When the beta update channel is live, the launcher surfaces an update prompt on a product card when a newer version exists.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When something fails

| What you see | What it means | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| A product card shows **Not installed** | That app isn't on this machine; the launcher doesn't bundle the products | Download its installer from [downloads.pidgeon.health](https://downloads.pidgeon.health), install, and the card flips to Installed. |
| An error banner about your session, with the product grid still visible | The account service couldn't be reached or the session expired | The grid never goes blank; entitlements show as unknown. Sign in again or retry once you're back online. |
| Cards stuck on **Checking…** | Installation detection is still resolving (or could not complete) | Give it a moment; if it persists, relaunch the launcher. The apps themselves still launch directly from the OS. |
